Coverage gaps

Schools with no report yet, by state. MS/HS by default, the same schools the headline counts (NCES level middle, high or secondary); Elementary is the new survey only; All is every school in the NCES list. "Lagging" = more than points below the national rate.

By state

One state at a time: which schools and districts are in the data, and which are not yet. "In the data" = at least one report (earlier waves carry forward for middle/high; elementary counts only with the new survey).

Elementary

The elementary survey is new this year, so elementary coverage restarts at zero. Schools that reported in earlier waves are listed as "reported before" and count once they take the new survey. Elementary is not part of the headline KPI. qualifier under review Elementary-only librarians currently never count because they answer no policy question; the team expects to change what counts for elementary.

Students

Teens report on their own middle or high school. A student report counts for coverage like any other valid report; this tab shows what students alone contribute.

Librarians

The librarian survey branches three ways: district (circulation only), elementary-only, and MS/HS (any librarian serving middle or high students, with or without elementary). Only the MS/HS branch answers the phone-policy questions, so only it counts for coverage. qualifier under review

Data quality

Coverage only counts valid reports. Everything excluded is listed so a rule that eats too much is visible the day it happens.
